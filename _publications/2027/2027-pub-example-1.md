abstract: >-
  This paper proposes a condition-preserving 3D diffusion robotic manipulation policy built on a three-stage Rewriting-Routing-Recovery pipeline, which addresses the issue of critical discriminative geometric information being lost during the compression of 3D observations in contact-sensitive long-horizon tasks, achieving consistent performance gains across multiple robotic manipulation benchmarks with only a tiny increase in model parameters.
