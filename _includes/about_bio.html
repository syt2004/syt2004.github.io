I am a member of the Robot Integration Laboratory, Faculty of Information Science and Technology, Beijing University of Technology. In the early stage of my undergraduate studies, my research focused on the applications of foundation models such as large language models and vision-language models. Later, I oriented my research toward embodied intelligence. Under the supervision of Lei Zhang, I work on robot dexterous manipulation, and under the guidance of Tongtong Feng, I conduct research on the generalization of human motion. These constitute my two primary research areas. Furthermore, I am applying for graduate positions. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in potential collaboration. My interests include Robotics, HCI, Computer Vision, and Multimodal Learning.
